Recommendation

Go with Open Door, and keep the Stamp for merch.

Open Door keeps most of what people already recognise: the red pin over green, read at a glance. It adds a clear, ownable idea, a local opening the door onto the mountains. It reads cleanly at 16 px, its one-colour version is strong, and the arch-and-peaks shape works across all three countries without leaning on any single national symbol.

The Caucasus Stamp works well alongside it as a secondary emblem for stickers, luggage tags and partner gifts, continuing the stamp tiles of the old site.

If you prefer a more premium, typographic look, Pin Wordmark is the alternative. Its serif wordmark could also be paired with the Open Door icon.

  1. Choose a direction, then refine it with a professional designer: optical corrections, final kerning and a proper type licence check.
  2. Before adoption, check for trademark conflicts (Sakpatenti in Georgia, plus EUIPO/WIPO for the EU markets) and register the final mark.
  3. Then roll out: website, favicon and social avatars first, followed by print and fair materials.

All lockups are vector with text in outlines. Fonts are open-licence (SIL OFL): Plus Jakarta Sans, Unbounded, Manrope, Outfit, Fraunces, Noto Sans Georgian. Files: design/logo/<concept>/{horizontal, stacked, icon, mono}.svg, plus reverse and white variants.
