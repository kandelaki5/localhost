## 3. Travelife

**Status: Travelife Partner (high confidence).**
- The public Travelife register (https://consumers.travelife.info/?lang=en#register) lists **"Local Host LLC", Georgia, level Partner, first approval 2025-11-26, valid until 2027-11-26**. You can check it with the register API: https://consumers.travelife.info/api/consumer-public-register?name=Local%20Host
- Other Georgian companies on the register, for context: VisitGeorgia and Georgian DMC are *Certified* (Jan 2026), and Promethea Voyages is a *Partner*.

**What the levels mean** (https://www.travelife.info/home/certification.html, https://consumers.travelife.info/?lang=en):
- **Travelife Engaged** is the preparatory phase: training, tools and the online platform. It is *not* a recognition level and gives **no right to use a Travelife recognition logo**.
- **Travelife Partner** is the first independently assessed recognition level. It covers about 60 mandatory criteria and is reassessed every 2 years. It is the pathway to Certified.
- **Travelife Certified** is the highest level: 200+ criteria and an extensive independent **on-site audit**, also reassessed every 2 years.

**Consistency check.** The company's Sustainability Report (Nov 2025, `docs/reference/pdf-text/report.txt`) follows the Travelife criteria for tour operators. It confirms a trained coordinator (Travelife basic training and exam). Many full-standard criteria still carry **target dates in 2026–2027**, for example 1.4 Collaboration (March 2027) and 1.6 Donations (April 2027). The PDF-to-text column layout is messy, so read dates for other criteria from the PDF itself. This fits Partner status, with Certified still a future goal.

**Safe wording for the site:**
> "Local Host is a **Travelife Partner**, independently assessed against the Travelife sustainability criteria for tour operators. Recognised since November 2025." (link: Travelife public register)

- **Badge:** the **Travelife Partner** logo only, from the Travelife Communication Guidelines and toolkit the company received. Link it to the register.
- ❌ Don't say "Travelife Certified", "certified sustainable", or make unqualified claims like "sustainable tours" or "eco-friendly holidays". Don't use the Certified logo.
- The template's "certificate screen" should show the **Partner** certificate. Ask the owner for the PDF or image.
- **EU rules:** Directive (EU) 2024/825, the "Empowering Consumers for the Green Transition" directive (EmpCo), applies from **27 Sept 2026**. Travelife itself warns that recognition "does not mean that companies can make unlimited or general claims" (https://www.travelife.info/home/certification-communication.html). This matters because the site targets PL/IT/DE consumers and agents. Keep claims specific: name the policy measures and the % reductions from the report.
- Re-check the status before Nov 2027.
