# Research: trade fairs ("Meet us at")

Researched 10 Oct 2026. Machine-readable version: `src/data/fairs.json`.

**Confidence key.**
- *High*: dates and venue confirmed on the fair's official site, or in the organiser's own press release.
- *Medium*: several consistent secondary sources, but the official site could not be checked.
- *Low*: a single or conflicting source.

"Last edition" is the most recent edition that had already started on 10 Oct 2026. "Next edition" is the first one starting after 10 Oct 2026.

This file only says when and where each fair takes place. Whether Local Host exhibits at a given edition is the owner's information (see BRIEF §1). Don't publish "see you at X 2027" unless the owner confirms it.

### BIT Milano: BIT – Borsa Internazionale del Turismo

- **Last edition:** **10-12 Feb 2026** (Tue-Thu). This was the first time BIT ran Tuesday to Thursday. Sources:
  - Quality Travel: https://www.qualitytravel.it/bit-2026-punta-sui-travel-makers-e-su-nuove-date-10-12-febbraio-dal-martedi-al-giovedi/172632

  - travel2fair: https://travel2fair.com/exhibitions/bit-international-tourism-exchange/9-february-2027-11-february-2027?page=2
  - Tradefest: https://tradefest.io/en/event/bit
  - EventsEye
  - Trade press also reports that Fiera Milano has set out its 2027 strategy: https://www.traveldailynews.com/fairs-exhibitions/bit-outlines-strategy-for-2027-edition/ (blocked to automated reading)

- **Confidence:** medium. The official page has not been checked. The 2027 venue is reported as Rho by one listing and as "Fiera Milano City" by EventsEye; Rho is more likely, since 2025 and 2026 were held there.

## Verification log (skeptical pass)

1. **BIT 2027 dates.** I tried to confirm them on the official site (blocked) and in Italian trade press. Only listing sites give 9-11 Feb 2027, though they all agree. One listing (EventsEye) names a different venue, Fiera Milano City → kept at **medium**.
2. **BMT 2027.** The official site shows a countdown ("144 giorni alla fiera" on 10 Oct 2026 = 3 Mar 2027) and the dates 3-5 Mar 2027. But its workshop page still shows "17-18 Marzo 2027". That looks like a leftover error; both date sets fall on the same weekdays. I kept the main fair dates, which also appear in the 2027 press release.
3. **ITB 2027.** The unusual mid-March date (16-18 Mar) made me check whether it was a listing error. It isn't: the official site gives the reason (Eid al-Fitr) and confirms that 2028 is 7-9 March.
4. **TT Warsaw edition number and venue.** Third-party pages call 2026 the "30th edition" (Blueprint Exhibits) or place it at "EXPO XXI" (JuFair). Both are out of date. The official site says **32nd edition** at **Ptak Warsaw Expo**. I used the official site.
5. **Tour Salon 2025 dates.** No official archive page was found, so the 3-5 Oct 2025 dates come from JETRO J-messe plus one exhibitor's post → medium for that field only.
6. **TTG.** The 2026 dates are checked on the official site. I also checked that the "13 14 15 October 2027" save-the-date really is for 2027, not a typo for 2026: the 2026 dates appear separately on the same page.
